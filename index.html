FAQ

Common questions

Answers to what clients and recruiters ask most frequently.

Are you available for freelance projects?
Yes! I'm currently available for freelance projects, both short-term and long-term. I work with clients globally and am comfortable in async-first, remote environments. Reach out via the contact form.
What's your typical project timeline?
It depends on scope. A simple landing page takes 3–5 days. A full web application with custom backend typically runs 4–8 weeks. I provide a detailed timeline after an initial discovery call.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Yes — I provide 30 days of post-launch support included with every project, covering bug fixes and minor updates. Extended maintenance plans are available for ongoing needs.
What tech stack do you prefer?
My preferred stack is React + TypeScript for frontend, Node.js + Express for backend, and MongoDB or PostgreSQL for data. I'm also comfortable with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Vercel deployments.
Are you open to full-time positions?
Absolutely. I'm actively exploring full-time opportunities, especially remote roles at product companies where I can grow into a senior or full-stack engineer position. Let's chat!
